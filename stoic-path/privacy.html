Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Stoic Path ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application.

1. Data Collection & CloudKit

Stoic Path uses Apple's CloudKit to synchronize your data (reflections, journal entries, and progress) across your personal devices. This data is stored securely in your private iCloud database. We do not have access to your private iCloud data.

2. Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data to improve the app's performance and user experience. This includes information like feature usage frequency and app crashes. No personally identifiable information (PII) is sent to Firebase.

3. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with Apple's StoreKit for processing subscriptions. Payment information is handled exclusively by Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy policy.

4. Your Choices

You can delete all your data at any time through the app's Settings menu. Since data is stored in your iCloud, you also have full control over it through your iOS device settings.
